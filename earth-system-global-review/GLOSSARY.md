# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Anomaly | Difference from a stated reference average or baseline |
| AOGCM | Atmosphere–ocean general circulation model |
| Argo | International array of profiling floats measuring ocean temperature and salinity |
| Bathymetry | Measurement and mapping of underwater depth |
| Climate normal | Standard reference average, commonly a 30-year period |
| CO₂-equivalent | Common unit for comparing the climate effect of different greenhouse gases |
| Cryosphere | Earth's frozen components: snow, sea ice, glaciers, ice sheets and permafrost |
| Earth energy imbalance | Difference between incoming absorbed energy and outgoing energy at the top of the atmosphere |
| Eruption | Volcanic release of lava, tephra, gas or other material |
| Extent | Total area of grid cells meeting a defined sea-ice concentration threshold |
| Gt | Gigatonne, one billion metric tonnes |
| GNSS | Global Navigation Satellite System; includes GPS and other constellations |
| GtCO₂ | Gigatonnes of carbon dioxide |
| Holocene | Current geological epoch, beginning about 11,700 years ago |
| Ice-sheet mass balance | Net gain or loss of mass of a continental ice sheet |
| InSAR | Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar; measures changes in radar range between repeat observations |
| Lithosphere | Rigid outer shell consisting of crust and mechanically strong uppermost mantle |
| Mantle | Thick silicate-rock region between crust and core |
| Multibeam sonar | Acoustic system used by ships to map a broad swath of seafloor |
| Ocean heat content | Energy stored in the ocean over a specified depth range and reference period |
| Ozone hole | Region with total-column ozone below a specified threshold, commonly 220 Dobson Units in Southern Hemisphere monitoring |
| Paleoclimate | Climate conditions reconstructed from natural archives before modern instrumental records |
| Permafrost | Ground that remains at or below 0 °C for at least two consecutive years |
| Plate tectonics | Theory describing movement and interaction of Earth's lithospheric plates |
| ppm | Parts per million |
| ppb | Parts per billion |
| ROV | Remotely operated underwater vehicle |
| Sea-ice area | Sum of ice-covered fraction within grid cells |
| Sea-ice extent | Total grid-cell area where ice concentration meets a chosen threshold |
| Seismic moment | Physical measure related to fault area, slip and rock rigidity; used to characterize earthquake size |
| Seamount | Underwater mountain, commonly volcanic in origin |
| Stratigraphy | Study and description of layered rocks and their sequence |
| Subduction | Process in which one tectonic plate descends beneath another |
| Thermal expansion | Increase in water volume as it warms |
| Tectonic deformation | Change in the shape or position of Earth's crust due to forces |
| TROPOMI | Satellite instrument measuring atmospheric trace gases |
| ZJ | Zettajoule, 10²¹ joules |

## Key distinctions

- **Weather** describes short-term atmospheric conditions; **climate** describes statistical patterns over longer periods.
- **Sea ice** floats; **land ice** rests on bedrock or land.
- **A volcano with Holocene activity** is not necessarily erupting now.
- **Volcanic alert level** and **aviation colour code** are different systems.
- **Magnitude** is an earthquake-size measure; **intensity** describes local shaking and effects.
- **Global mean sea level** and **relative coastal sea level** are different.
- **Core inference** from seismic waves is not direct imaging.

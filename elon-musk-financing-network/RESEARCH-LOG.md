## Evidence status vocabulary

- **CONFIRMED_PERSONAL_LENDER:** exact legal lender directly identified as creditor to Musk or the trust.
- **CONFIRMED_CORPORATE_LENDER:** exact legal lender identified in a company credit agreement.
- **DEBT_SECURITY_PURCHASE:** lender/investor purchased company debt securities; not a bank loan to Musk.
- **AGENT_OR_TRUSTEE:** administrative or fiduciary role; not automatically a lender.
- **LITIGATION_ALLEGATION:** claim made by a party in litigation, not a judicial finding.
- **LEAD_UNVERIFIED:** plausible investigative route without direct proof.
- **NOT_FOUND_IN_REVIEWED_RECORDS:** no evidence found in the documents reviewed; not proof of nonexistence.

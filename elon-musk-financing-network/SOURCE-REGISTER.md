## Source-use rules

1. Cite the specific filing or executed agreement, not only an institution's home page.
2. Record filing date separately from transaction date and balance date.
3. Treat a prospectus as an issuer disclosure; it does not substitute for the underlying private loan contract.
4. Treat allegations in complaints as allegations unless a court adopts them as findings.
5. Distinguish legal entities within a financial group.
6. Preserve the exact language used for lender counts and aggregate amounts.
7. If a source is not fully inspected, mark it as a lead rather than as verified evidence.

## Key extracted passages

### S01 - 2015 personal borrowing
The prospectus says Goldman Sachs Bank USA had made $275 million in aggregate credit extensions to Musk and the revocable trust beginning in June 2011. It separately identifies a $200 million aggregate Morgan Stanley Smith Barney loan to Musk. The loans were full recourse and secured by Tesla and unrelated-entity shares held by Musk and the trust.

### S02 - 2016 unnamed lender
The prospectus says Musk received an approximately $187 million loan from another financial institution not involved in that offering. It says the loans were secured by portions of Tesla stock and other shares of unrelated entities owned by Musk and the trust.

### S03 - Tesla corporate facility
The February 2016 amendment sets total revolving commitments at $1 billion. Its schedule assigns $147.5 million to JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and $100 million to Wells Fargo Bank, National Association. These are Tesla borrower commitments, not personal Musk credit.

### S04 - SolarCity corporate lender group
The April 2016 amendment names Bank of America, Credit Suisse, Silicon Valley Bank, Western Alliance, CIT Bank, American Savings Bank, Deutsche Bank, Royal Bank of Canada, Citibank and Goldman Sachs Bank USA as lenders, with Bank of America as administrative agent.

### S05 - 2017 unnamed aggregate
The prospectus reports approximately $279.9 million in aggregate loans from other financial institutions as of 28 February 2017, secured by Tesla shares held by Musk and the trust. It does not name the lenders or disclose the individual allocations.

### S06 - SolarCity related-party debt
The proxy statement identifies the $10 million zero-coupon convertible note to the trust, $65 million of 6.50% Solar Bonds issued to Musk and exchanged for a $65 million promissory note in April 2017, and SpaceX's purchases of $90 million and $75 million of 4.40% Solar Bonds in 2016.

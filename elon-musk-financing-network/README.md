## Core findings at this snapshot

1. SEC filings identify **Goldman Sachs Bank USA** and **Morgan Stanley Smith Barney LLC** as direct personal-credit providers to Musk and/or his revocable trust.
2. Tesla's May 2016 prospectus reports an additional **approximately $187.0 million** loan from one unnamed financial institution. It says the lender was not involved in that offering.
3. Tesla's March 2017 prospectus reports **approximately $279.9 million in aggregate** from other financial institutions as of 28 February 2017. The institutions are not named. The 2016 and 2017 figures are not added together.
4. **JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.** and **Wells Fargo Bank, National Association** are confirmed lenders under Tesla's corporate asset-based revolving credit facility, with commitments of **$147.5 million** and **$100 million**, respectively, in the February 2016 amendment. These are corporate commitments, not personal loans to Musk.
5. SolarCity's April 2016 credit-agreement amendment identifies a separate lender group. This is corporate borrowing by SolarCity, not proof that any member of that group lent personally to Musk.
6. SolarCity issued a $10 million zero-coupon convertible note to the Elon Musk Revocable Trust in December 2015 and $65 million of 6.50% Solar Bonds to Musk in August 2016; the latter were exchanged for a $65 million promissory note in April 2017.
7. SpaceX purchased $90 million and $75 million of SolarCity 4.40% Solar Bonds in March and June 2016. These were corporate debt investments by SpaceX, not personal loans by Musk.

## Research discipline

- Separate personal borrowing, corporate facilities, debt-security purchases, equity purchases, donations, and ordinary commercial payments.
- Record legal entity names, not only banking brands.
- Distinguish facility commitments from actual drawn balances and outstanding principal.
- Do not infer that a bank lent personally to Musk merely because it lent to Tesla or SolarCity.
- Do not add the $187.0 million and $279.9 million disclosures without documentary proof that they are separate, simultaneously outstanding balances.
- Label direct facts, issuer disclosures, litigation allegations, analytical leads, and unresolved questions separately.
- Keep updating this dossier as new records are verified.

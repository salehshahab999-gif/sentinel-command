# Element 024: Chromium

**Symbol:** Cr  
**Atomic number:** 24  
**Group:** 6  
**Period:** 4  
**Block:** d-block  
**Classification:** Transition metal  
**Standard atomic weight (CIAAW 2024):** 51.9961(6)  
**Ground-state electron configuration:** [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹  
**Physical state at approximately 298 K and 1 bar:** Solid

## 1. Atomic and periodic context
Chromium is element 24. Its ground-state electron configuration, [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹, is the conventional starting point for interpreting valence, bonding, and periodic behavior. Transition-metal chemistry is particularly sensitive to oxidation state, coordination environment, ligand field, and phase.

## 2. Isotopic and nuclear context
Chromium has several stable isotopes. Its chemistry spans multiple oxidation states. Cr(III) and Cr(VI) compounds differ substantially in toxicity and environmental behavior; Cr(VI) compounds are recognized carcinogenic hazards. Standard atomic weight summarizes naturally occurring material and is not interchangeable with the mass of any individual nuclide. Isotope-specific masses, abundances, and decay data belong in the dedicated nuclear-data tables.

## 3. Chemical and materials significance
Stainless steels, hard chromium coatings, pigments, corrosion-resistant alloys, and catalysts are major applications.

## 4. Data quality and interpretation
The standard atomic weight is reproduced from the CIAAW 2024 table. Parenthetical digits indicate uncertainty in the final quoted digits. The electron configuration is the conventional ground-state configuration. Physical state is stated at approximately 298 K and 1 bar.

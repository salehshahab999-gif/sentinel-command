# Element 002: Helium

**Symbol:** He  
**Atomic number:** 2  
**Group:** 18  
**Period:** 1  
**Block:** p-block  
**Classification:** Noble gas  
**Standard atomic weight (CIAAW 2024):** 4.002602(2)  
**Ground-state electron configuration:** 1s²  
**Physical state at approximately 298 K and 1 bar:** Gas

## 1. Atomic and periodic context
Helium is element 2 in the periodic table. Its ground-state configuration, 1s², provides the starting point for interpreting its valence structure, bonding patterns, and characteristic chemistry. Group placement is a useful organizing framework, but observed chemistry also depends on molecular environment, oxidation state, and thermodynamic conditions.

## 2. Isotopic and nuclear context
Both naturally occurring stable isotopes, ³He and ⁴He, are important in low-temperature physics; ⁴He exhibits superfluidity below its lambda transition. A complete isotope dossier requires nuclide-by-nuclide evaluation of atomic masses, natural abundances, decay modes, and half-lives. Values are not inferred from the standard atomic weight.

## 3. Chemical and materials significance
Cryogenic refrigeration, inert-atmosphere processing, leak detection, gas chromatography, and helium–neon lasers.

## 4. Data quality and interpretation
The standard atomic weight is reproduced from the CIAAW 2024 table. Bracketed values are intervals reflecting the accepted range for normal terrestrial materials, not a symmetric measurement uncertainty. Parenthetical digits denote uncertainty in the final quoted digits. The electron configuration is the conventional ground-state configuration; exceptional configurations and spectroscopic term data require element-specific evaluation.

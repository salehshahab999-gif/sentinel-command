# Element 007: Nitrogen

**Symbol:** N  
**Atomic number:** 7  
**Group:** 15  
**Period:** 2  
**Block:** p-block [4]
**Classification:** Nonmetal  
**Standard atomic weight (CIAAW 2024):** [14.00643, 14.00728] [1]
**Ground-state electron configuration:** [He] 2s² 2p³   [3]
**Physical state at approximately 298 K and 1 bar:** Gas [4]

## 1. Atomic and periodic context
Nitrogen is element 7 in the periodic table. Its ground-state configuration, [He] 2s² 2p³, provides the starting point for interpreting its valence structure, bonding patterns, and characteristic chemistry. Group placement is a useful organizing framework, but observed chemistry also depends on molecular environment, oxidation state, and thermodynamic conditions.

## 2. Isotopic and nuclear context
Atmospheric nitrogen is predominantly N₂, whose strong triple bond gives the molecule substantial kinetic stability. The stable isotopes are ¹⁴N and ¹⁵N. A complete isotope dossier requires nuclide-by-nuclide evaluation of atomic masses, natural abundances, decay modes, and half-lives. Values are not inferred from the standard atomic weight.

## 3. Chemical and materials significance
Ammonia and fertilizer manufacture, inerting and blanketing, cryogenic cooling as liquid nitrogen, and specialty chemical synthesis.

## 4. Data quality and interpretation
The standard atomic weight is reproduced from the CIAAW 2024 table. Bracketed values are intervals reflecting the accepted range for normal terrestrial materials, not a symmetric measurement uncertainty. Parenthetical digits denote uncertainty in the final quoted digits. The electron configuration is the conventional ground-state configuration; exceptional configurations and spectroscopic term data require element-specific evaluation.

# Element 012: Magnesium

**Symbol:** Mg  
**Atomic number:** 12  
**Group:** 2  
**Period:** 3  
**Block:** s-block  
**Classification:** Alkaline-earth metal  
**Standard atomic weight (CIAAW 2024):** [24.304, 24.307] [1]
**Ground-state electron configuration:** [Ne] 3s²   [3]
**Ground-state term symbol:** ¹S₀ [3]  
**Physical state at approximately 298 K and 1 bar:** Solid [4]

## 1. Atomic and periodic context
Magnesium is element 12. Its valence configuration, [Ne] 3s², provides the first-order framework for understanding bonding, oxidation-state preferences, and periodic trends. Group trends are guides rather than substitutes for compound-specific thermodynamic and spectroscopic evidence.

## 2. Isotopic and nuclear context
Natural magnesium contains ²⁴Mg, ²⁵Mg, and ²⁶Mg. Magnesium isotopes are widely used in geochemistry and cosmochemistry. Fine magnesium powder and molten metal present significant fire hazards. The standard atomic weight is not a nuclide mass and must not be used as a substitute for isotope-specific nuclear data. A complete nuclear appendix will report nuclide masses, natural abundances, decay modes, and half-lives separately.

## 3. Chemical and materials significance
Lightweight structural alloys, die casting, pyrotechnic compositions, sacrificial anodes, and organomagnesium reagents are major application areas. [4]

## 4. Data quality and interpretation
The standard atomic weight is taken from the CIAAW 2024 table. An interval denotes the range expected for normal terrestrial materials; parenthetical digits indicate uncertainty in the final quoted digits. The electron configuration is the conventional ground-state configuration. Physical state is stated at approximately 298 K and 1 bar.

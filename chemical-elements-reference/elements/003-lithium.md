# Element 003: Lithium

**Symbol:** Li  
**Atomic number:** 3  
**Group:** 1  
**Period:** 2  
**Block:** s-block [4]
**Classification:** Alkali metal  
**Standard atomic weight (CIAAW 2024):** [6.938, 6.997] [1]
**Ground-state electron configuration:** [He] 2s¹   [3]
**Physical state at approximately 298 K and 1 bar:** Solid [4]

## 1. Atomic and periodic context
Lithium is element 3 in the periodic table. Its ground-state configuration, [He] 2s¹, provides the starting point for interpreting its valence structure, bonding patterns, and characteristic chemistry. Group placement is a useful organizing framework, but observed chemistry also depends on molecular environment, oxidation state, and thermodynamic conditions.

## 2. Isotopic and nuclear context
Natural lithium is principally ⁶Li and ⁷Li. Its standard atomic-weight interval reflects natural isotopic variation; lithium chemistry is dominated by Li⁺. A complete isotope dossier requires nuclide-by-nuclide evaluation of atomic masses, natural abundances, decay modes, and half-lives. Values are not inferred from the standard atomic weight.

## 3. Chemical and materials significance
Rechargeable lithium-ion batteries, specialty glass and ceramics, lubricating greases, and selected pharmaceutical compounds.

## 4. Data quality and interpretation
The standard atomic weight is reproduced from the CIAAW 2024 table. Bracketed values are intervals reflecting the accepted range for normal terrestrial materials, not a symmetric measurement uncertainty. Parenthetical digits denote uncertainty in the final quoted digits. The electron configuration is the conventional ground-state configuration; exceptional configurations and spectroscopic term data require element-specific evaluation.

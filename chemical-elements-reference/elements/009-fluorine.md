# Element 009: Fluorine

**Symbol:** F  
**Atomic number:** 9  
**Group:** 17  
**Period:** 2  
**Block:** p-block  
**Classification:** Halogen  
**Standard atomic weight (CIAAW 2024):** 18.998403162(5)  
**Ground-state electron configuration:** [He] 2s² 2p⁵  
**Physical state at approximately 298 K and 1 bar:** Gas

## 1. Atomic and periodic context
Fluorine is element 9 in the periodic table. Its ground-state configuration, [He] 2s² 2p⁵, provides the starting point for interpreting its valence structure, bonding patterns, and characteristic chemistry. Group placement is a useful organizing framework, but observed chemistry also depends on molecular environment, oxidation state, and thermodynamic conditions.

## 2. Isotopic and nuclear context
Elemental fluorine (F₂) is extremely reactive, toxic, and corrosive. Fluoride chemistry is extensive, but hazards depend strongly on chemical form and exposure route. A complete isotope dossier requires nuclide-by-nuclide evaluation of atomic masses, natural abundances, decay modes, and half-lives. Values are not inferred from the standard atomic weight.

## 3. Chemical and materials significance
Fluorochemicals, fluoropolymers, uranium conversion chemistry, semiconductor processing, and fluorides used in dental materials.

## 4. Data quality and interpretation
The standard atomic weight is reproduced from the CIAAW 2024 table. Bracketed values are intervals reflecting the accepted range for normal terrestrial materials, not a symmetric measurement uncertainty. Parenthetical digits denote uncertainty in the final quoted digits. The electron configuration is the conventional ground-state configuration; exceptional configurations and spectroscopic term data require element-specific evaluation.

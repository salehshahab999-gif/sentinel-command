# Element 025: Manganese

**Symbol:** Mn  
**Atomic number:** 25  
**Group:** 7  
**Period:** 4  
**Block:** d-block  
**Classification:** Transition metal  
**Standard atomic weight (CIAAW 2024):** 54.938 043(2)  
**Ground-state electron configuration:** [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s²  
**Physical state at approximately 298 K and 1 bar:** Solid

## 1. Atomic and periodic context
Manganese is element 25. Its ground-state electron configuration, [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s², is the conventional starting point for interpreting valence, bonding, and periodic behavior. Transition-metal chemistry is particularly sensitive to oxidation state, coordination environment, ligand field, and phase.

## 2. Isotopic and nuclear context
Naturally occurring manganese is effectively monoisotopic as ⁵⁵Mn. Its many oxidation states support extensive oxide chemistry and catalytic redox cycles. Excessive exposure, especially by inhalation, can cause neurotoxicity. Standard atomic weight summarizes naturally occurring material and is not interchangeable with the mass of any individual nuclide. Isotope-specific masses, abundances, and decay data belong in the dedicated nuclear-data tables.

## 3. Chemical and materials significance
Steelmaking, batteries, pigments, catalysts, and agricultural micronutrient formulations are important uses.

## 4. Data quality and interpretation
The standard atomic weight is reproduced from the CIAAW 2024 table. Parenthetical digits indicate uncertainty in the final quoted digits. The electron configuration is the conventional ground-state configuration. Physical state is stated at approximately 298 K and 1 bar.

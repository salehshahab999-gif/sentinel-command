# Research Source Register

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-18  
**Purpose:** Supplement the authoritative evaluated databases with peer-reviewed primary research, doctoral theses, and active university/national-laboratory research outputs.  
**Scope at this snapshot:** Initial discovery for elements 1–20 and cross-cutting nuclear-data methodology. A source is not automatically authoritative for every property of an element; each citation must be attached only to the claim it actually supports.

## Source-selection policy

1. **Controlling numerical authorities remain property-specific.** CIAAW controls standard atomic weights and representative isotope compositions; NIST ASD controls the adopted atomic-spectroscopy records cited for ionization energies and level designations; ENSDF/NuDat and NUBASE are evaluated nuclear-data resources.
2. **Primary research papers** are used for novel measurements, methods, and element-specific findings. Record the sample, instrument, calibration, conditions, uncertainty budget, and whether the result has been independently reproduced.
3. **Doctoral theses** are valuable for detailed apparatus descriptions, calibration protocols, systematic-error budgets, and extensive bibliographies. A thesis is not treated as peer-reviewed journal evidence merely because it was awarded a PhD. Its claims must be checked against later papers and evaluated datasets.
4. **Research-group pages** identify active investigators and current work but are discovery sources, not evidence for numerical claims unless they link to a citable paper or dataset.
5. **Citation counts are not a quality verdict.** Check correction/retraction notices, later contradictory work, and the exact scope of each cited result.
6. **Date discipline:** distinguish the publication date of a paper from the version/date of the dataset used in the manuscript. Do not describe a 2021 evaluation as the latest 2026 dataset unless a new release is verified.

## Selected scholarly records discovered

### Hydrogen (Z = 1)

**Scheidegger, Simon Emanuel (2023).** *Precision spectroscopy of Rydberg states of the hydrogen atom*. Doctoral thesis, ETH Zürich.  
Stable record/DOI: https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000640084  
Repository full text: https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/448f93e5-a4a3-4b7f-aa6a-ebe35b018d9c/content  
**Potential use:** precision transition-frequency measurement, electric-field Stark shifts, systematic-error treatment, and determination of the hydrogen ionization frequency/Rydberg constant. Use as detailed experimental-method evidence; cross-check adopted constants against current CODATA/NIST recommendations.

### Helium (Z = 2)

**Hassan Rezaeian, Nima (2015).** *A Precise Few-Nucleon Size Difference by Isotope Shift Measurements of Helium*. PhD thesis, University of North Texas.  
Repository full text: https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc804828/m2/1/high_res_d/dissertation.pdf  
**Potential use:** isotope-shift measurements between ³He and ⁴He, laser spectroscopy, frequency control, and nuclear charge-radius extraction. Thesis reports its own uncertainty decomposition; preserve the distinction between experimental and theoretical uncertainties.

**van der Werf, Yuri (2023).** *Probing Nuclear Sizes through Precision Spectroscopy of Ultracold Bosonic and Fermionic Helium*. PhD thesis, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5463/thesis.364  
Institutional record: https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/probing-nuclear-sizes-through-precision-spectroscopy-of-ultracold/  
**Potential use:** precision spectroscopy in ultracold ³He/⁴He and nuclear charge-radius differences; current academic thesis and a useful research-group trace.

**Gründeman, Elmer Louis (2024).** *Laser excitation of the 1S–2S transition in He+: Towards precision spectroscopy in an ion trap*. PhD thesis, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5463/thesis.872  
Institutional record: https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/laser-excitation-of-the-1s-2s-transition-in-he-towards-precision-/  
**Potential use:** He⁺ precision spectroscopy, QED/nuclear-size corrections, frequency-comb and Ramsey-comb methods. Important qualification: the thesis describes steps toward a precision measurement; do not cite it as proof that the target transition has already been measured to the proposed precision.

### Beryllium (Z = 4)

**Dickopf, Stefan et al. (2024).** “Precision spectroscopy on ⁹Be overcomes limitations from nuclear structure.” *Nature*.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07795-1  
Publisher page: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07795-1  
**Potential use:** high-precision hyperfine/Zeeman spectroscopy of hydrogen-like ⁹Be³⁺, nuclear magnetic moment, Zemach radius, and QED/nuclear-structure calculations. A publisher correction dated 10 September 2024 is indicated on the article record; inspect and cite the corrected version where relevant.

**Sasidharan, Sangeetha.** *High-precision atomic mass measurement of helium-4*. Heidelberg University repository record: https://archiv.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/volltextserver/33938/  
**Potential use:** Penning-trap mass spectrometry and mass-measurement systematics for ⁴He. Confirm full bibliographic metadata and final award/publication details before formal citation.

### Boron (Z = 5)

**Aramendía, Maite et al.** “Boron elemental and isotopic determination via the BF diatomic molecule using high-resolution continuum source graphite furnace molecular absorption spectrometry.”  
Repository/full-text record: https://zaguan.unizar.es/record/132219/files/texto_completo.pdf  
**Potential use:** experimental boron isotope measurement by molecular absorption, instrumental interferences, isotope-ratio precision/accuracy, and comparison with ICP-MS. Confirm final journal metadata and DOI from the publisher record before using as a formal bibliography entry.

**Li, Gang (2012).** *Spectroscopy of small boron-containing molecules and line intensity calculations for HCl and CaH*. Doctoral thesis, White Rose eTheses Online (University of Leeds / Sheffield / York repository consortium).  
Repository: https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/2813/  
**Potential use:** high-resolution spectroscopy of BO isotopologues and boron-containing molecules. Use for molecular spectroscopy only, not as a general authority for elemental boron properties.

### Oxygen and fluorine (Z = 8–9)

**Almusidi, Tahani Hamad (2022).** *Quasi-Free Scattering off Neutron-Rich Oxygen and Fluorine Isotopes at Relativistic Beam Energies at R3B*. PhD thesis, University of York.  
Repository full text: https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/31774/1/Almusidi_204050671_CorrectedThesisClean.pdf  
**Potential use:** neutron-rich oxygen and fluorine isotope structure, reaction channels, cross sections, and comparison of measured momentum distributions with reaction theory. This is nuclear-physics evidence for the studied nuclides, not natural terrestrial isotope-abundance evidence.

### Neon (Z = 10)

**Riley, Joscelin E. (2017).** *A Study of ¹⁹Ne Resonances and their Astrophysical Implication for the Detection of Novae*. PhD thesis, University of York.  
Repository full text: https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/20010/7/jeRiley.pdf  
**Potential use:** ¹⁹Ne nuclear levels/resonance parameters, astrophysical reaction rates, and experimental nuclear spectroscopy. Do not confuse radioactive ¹⁹Ne with the stable isotopes used in CIAAW natural-abundance tables.

### Cross-cutting evaluated nuclear data (all elements)

**Kondev, F. G., Wang, M., Huang, W. J., Naimi, S., and Audi, G. (2021).** “The NUBASE2020 evaluation of nuclear physics properties.” *Chinese Physics C* 45, 030001.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1137/abddae  
Publisher: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1674-1137/abddae  
IAEA/AMDC PDF: https://www-nds.iaea.org/amdc/ame2020/NUBASE2020.pdf  
**Potential use:** evaluated masses, isomeric excitation energies, half-lives, spin/parity, decay modes/intensities, and bibliography. Nuclide-specific current ENSDF/NuDat records and newer papers must be checked before release.

## Search limitations and quality notes

- OpenAlex topic searches can return semantically adjacent but irrelevant papers, especially for broad phrases such as “materials properties.” Such results are not admitted to the bibliography until title, abstract, source, and property relevance are checked.
- A doctoral thesis may be excellent evidence for method and experimental detail, but its numerical result must be reconciled with later peer-reviewed work and current evaluated databases.
- The Scite MCP literature-verification service was not accessible during this snapshot because the connected account requires a paid plan or active trial. Therefore no claim is made that Scite has checked the cited papers for retractions, corrections, or contrasting citations. Publisher pages and DOI records must be checked directly in the relevant audit phase.
- This register is a discovery and provenance aid, not a substitute for the element-by-element property bibliography. Only sources actually used in a monograph should be cited in that monograph's claim-level references.

# Research Note - 3 October 2026

This public toolkit grew out of a live, multi-source OSINT investigation conducted on 3 October 2026.

During the publicly indexed search performed for that investigation, no independently published open-data geolocation result was found that was clearly more precise than the working convergence documented in the associated Riyadh research record.

This note does **not** claim that no other person in the world found a similar result. It records what was found during the documented public search.

The methodological contribution is the combination of:

- open satellite data;
- local and regional reporting;
- original-media provenance;
- weather and wind constraints;
- solar/shadow geometry;
- geographic cross-checking;
- explicit uncertainty handling;
- reproducible source logging;
- automated validation.

The purpose is to make the workflow reusable so the next researcher can continue from the evidence base instead of spending hours rebuilding the same source map.

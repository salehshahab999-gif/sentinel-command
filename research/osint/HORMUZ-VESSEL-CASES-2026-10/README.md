# Strait of Hormuz Vessel Incident Research (October 2026)

This directory indexes two separate OSINT working dossiers. They are intentionally kept separate from the GEM NO.2 / UAE coastal oil-spill case.

## Case files

1. [Unidentified crude-oil supertanker, reported 10 October 2026](../HORMUZ-UNKNOWN-SUPERTANKER-2026-10/README.md)  
   Iranian reporting attributed to the IRGC Navy describes a supertanker mine-strike and fire. Vessel identity and exact location remain unknown. A UKMTO-reported unknown-projectile incident may be related, but that linkage is not established.

2. [NV SUNSHINE LPG carrier, allegation dated 9 October 2026](../NV-SUNSHINE-HORMUZ-2026-10/README.md)  
   The IRGC Navy allegation is documented alongside the 10 October PVTrans denial, which says the vessel was not attacked and continued normal operations.

## Methodological rules

- Distinguish a reported claim from an independently verified event.
- Preserve contradictory primary statements rather than choosing one without evidence.
- Do not infer a ship identity from vessel class or cargo capacity alone.
- Do not merge incidents based only on proximity or similar descriptions.
- Mark secondary AIS and satellite interpretations as secondary until raw data and metadata are archived.
- Record all event times in UTC and retain original local-time wording where supplied.

## Current limitations

Neither dossier currently contains independently downloaded and processed raw AIS messages, raw satellite products, or original video files. The dossiers document the sources found and the next verification steps; they do not claim those data-processing tasks are complete.

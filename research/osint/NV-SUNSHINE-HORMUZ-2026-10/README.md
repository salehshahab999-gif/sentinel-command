**Method:** Open-source intelligence (OSINT)

## Executive summary

On 9 October 2026, Tasnim reported an IRGC Navy statement alleging that the LPG carrier **NV SUNSHINE**, associated in the statement with Nhat Viet, had been hit while attempting to transit a route south of the Strait of Hormuz. The report alleged a fire in the engine room and propulsion system.

On 10 October 2026, **PetroVietnam Transportation Corporation (PVTrans)** published a direct rebuttal. PVTrans said it had instructed NVTrans, the vessel's direct owner/manager/operator, to verify the report with the captain and relevant parties. PVTrans stated that the vessel had **not** been attacked, that crew and cargo were safe, and that the vessel continued normal operations according to schedule.

The operator's direct denial is material and must be presented alongside the Iranian claim. The available sources do not support describing an attack on NV SUNSHINE as an established fact. The case is best classified as a **disputed attack allegation, denied by the operating company**.

## Assessment

**Current assessment:** The attack allegation is contradicted by a direct statement from the vessel's corporate operator. The available public evidence in this dossier does not independently establish an attack, fire, or damage to NV SUNSHINE.

This is not proof that every detail of the vessel's voyage is independently verified. The PVTrans statement is a primary company account, while the AIS particulars and track snippet are secondary. Raw AIS and independent imagery have not been downloaded and processed in this research stage.

## Evidence grading

- **High:** direct official company statement for what PVTrans says it verified and the operational status it reports.
- **Medium:** attributable Iranian statement and reputable reporting that the allegation was made.
- **Low / secondary:** third-party vessel particulars and AIS-monitoring summary until raw records are archived.
- **Unverified:** exact incident coordinate, alleged damage/fire, and any link between the allegation and other Strait of Hormuz incidents.

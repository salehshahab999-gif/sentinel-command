## 🧭 Interpretation notes

- **Temperature:** the 2025 anomaly is 0.12°C below 2024, but that single-year difference does not reverse the long-term warming trend. 2024 remains the warmest year in the WMO 175-year record.
- **Greenhouse gases:** atmospheric concentrations continued to rise in 2025. These are concentration observations, not annual emission totals.
- **Fossil CO₂:** the Global Carbon Budget's 2025 growth estimate is reported as +1.0%. The published displayed values are rounded, so recomputing the percentage from 37.8 and 38.1 GtCO₂ will not reproduce the source-reported rate.
- **Regional anomalies:** country-specific normals remain separate from the global 1850–1900 series.
- **Sea ice:** NASA/NSIDC and NIPR products remain separate and are never averaged.
- **Percentages:** never calculate percentage changes for temperature anomalies or precipitation departures. Use absolute or percentage-point changes.

## 🔁 Annual update cycle

GitHub Actions opens an annual review issue on 15 April at 08:23 UTC, requesting the previous complete calendar year's authoritative datasets. New numbers enter the report only after a primary source, product definition, period, unit, baseline and uncertainty have been checked. The visual dashboard and comparison tables are then regenerated automatically.

The scheduled issue is a review trigger. It does not assume that every institution has published its annual report by that date.
